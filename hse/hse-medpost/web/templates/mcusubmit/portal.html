Send a worker's medical checkup result to the medical team as a PDF, and follow what happens to it.

Sign in Request an account

  3. Send results. One PDF per worker. You can see the status of each one.

    This triggers cascading database deletes and cannot be undone. Deleting the account also deletes, in one step:

    • all of their {{.Foot.Documents}} submitted documents, whatever their status (pending, reviewed, closed), so reviewers will no longer see them in the Submitted tab;
    • their {{.Foot.Batches}} batches and {{.Foot.Files}} stored PDF files;
    • their sign-in, setup and email-change links.
