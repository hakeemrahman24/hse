Request a permit for a worker or a visitor, or check a permit you already have. You do not need an account.

For a Work Entry Permit the worker needs a valid medical checkup (MCU) result of Fit or Fit with note.

Medical checkup (MCU) submission

For companies. Send each worker's medical checkup result as a PDF, then follow its status.

The medical team approves new company accounts. You get an email to choose your login.

How it works

  1. The company sends the medical checkup (MCU)One PDF per worker. The medical team checks it.
  2. Request the permitOnly for a worker whose MCU is Fit or Fit with note.
  3. Show your card at the gateApproved workers get a permit card with a QR code.

Request an entry permit

Choose the kind of permit. You do not need to sign in. Have the documents ready first, so you can finish in one go.

Work Entry Permit

For a worker who will work on site.

You need: the worker's name and date of birth, a valid medical checkup (MCU) result of Fit or Fit with note, the ID card (KTP) or passport, an ID photo, the contract and the certificate of competence.

Request a Work Entry Permit

Visitor Entry Permit

For a short visit. The visitor does not need an MCU, but answers a health declaration.

You need: the visitor's details, the ID card (KTP) or passport, an ID photo and the appointment letter.

Request a Visitor Entry Permit
