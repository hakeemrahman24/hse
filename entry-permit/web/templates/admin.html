Gate officers

Pick the officer's name from the eligible workers (Fit or Fit with note), give a username and a PIN. On the scanner page an officer signs in with the username and the PIN. An officer whose MCU stops being Fit or Fit with note is archived automatically (checked every few hours); restoring is up to you. PINs are stored scrambled and cannot be shown again: set a new PIN if one is forgotten. Two officers may have the same PIN. Archive an officer to stop their access but keep the record; remove deletes them for good.
